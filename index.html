A free breadboard for every circuit you want to try.

Benchnote gives every electrical and electronic engineering student a virtual lab bench: a digital trainer with real 74-series chips and an analog trainer with a power supply, function generator, meters and an oscilloscope. Place parts, drag wires and test any circuit you like, free, on a laptop or a phone. Common lab experiments come already built as examples, so you can see one wired before you try it yourself.
