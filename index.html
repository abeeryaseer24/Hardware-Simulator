Practise the lab before you walk into it.

Benchnote is a free virtual lab for electrical and electronic engineering students. Every experiment is shown fully wired on a breadboard, with an empty board right beside it so you can build it yourself and have it checked. Digital logic with 74-series chips, analog circuits with real instruments, and a K-map solver, all in one place, on a laptop or a phone.

Start with digital Start with analog
Digital Lab Gates, adders, MUX, decoders, flip-flops and counters on a digital trainer. Analog Lab Network theorems, RC and RLC, diodes, transistors and op-amps with meters and a scope. K-Map Solver Minimal SOP and POS for up to six variables, with the working shown.

Designed and developed by Talha Yaseer Abeer.

Free to use for learning and lab preparation.
