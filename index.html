The night before a lab exam

  1. Read Breadboard 101. Ten minutes there saves the classic mistakes: a chip on one side of the channel, power on the wrong pin, a split rail.
  2. Build each experiment with the guide on. The dashed outlines show where every chip and wire goes, and the steps tick off as you wire.
  3. Clear the board and build it again from memory. Then press “Check my circuit”. Red rows tell you exactly which input combination fails.
  4. Break it on purpose. Pull the ground wire, short two outputs, leave an input floating. Knowing what the fault looks like is half of debugging in the lab.
  5. Skim the revision sheet. Truth tables, excitation tables and pinouts are what viva questions are made of.
